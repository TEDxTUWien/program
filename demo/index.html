How to find us

Getting to the Kuppelsaal

  1. 1

    Arrive at Karlsplatz

    The U-Bahn lines U1, U2 and U4 stop at Karlsplatz, right next to the TU Wien main building.

  2. 2

    Find the main building

    TU Wien, Karlsplatz 13, 1040 Vienna — enter through the main entrance.

  3. 3

    Go up to the Kuppelsaal

    The hall is in Stiege 1 on the 4th floor, right under the dome.

  4. 4

    Check in and enjoy

    Doors open at 10:00, the first session starts at 11:00.
